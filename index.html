Q1 (Physics): Ohm's Law & Resistance Factors (5.0 Marks)

State Ohm's Law (V = IR) and explain how wire length, cross-sectional area, and resistivity affect conductor resistance.

Marking Key: Statement at constant temperature (2.5M) + Proportionalities (R ∝ L, R ∝ 1/A, R = ρL/A) (2.5M).

Explain reaction conditions, balanced formula for hydrated ferric oxide, and two prevention methods.

Marking Key: 4Fe + 3O2 + 2xH2O -> 2Fe2O3.xH2O (2.5M) + Galvanization (zinc coating) & Painting (2.5M).

Q3 (Chemistry): Neutralization Reaction & Antacids (5.0 Marks)

Define acid-base neutralization, provide equation (HCl + NaOH), and explain Milk of Magnesia [Mg(OH)2] action.

Marking Key: Acid + Base -> Salt + Water (2.5M) + Magnesium Hydroxide neutralizing excess hydrochloric acid (2.5M).

Q1: Ohm's Law and Resistance of a Conductor

Ohm's Law states that at constant temperature, current I is directly proportional to potential difference V (V = I * R).

Factors: 1. Length (R ∝ L) 2. Area of cross-section (R ∝ 1/A) 3. Material resistivity (R = ρ * L / A).

Q2: Rusting of Iron (Corrosion)

Iron reacts with oxygen and moisture to form rust: 4Fe(s) + 3O2(g) + 2xH2O(l) -> 2Fe2O3.xH2O.

Prevention: Galvanization (coating with zinc) and painting.
